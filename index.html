02 / RESEARCHTHREE CONNECTED LINES OF INQUIRY

Research.

I study how digital platforms and artificial intelligence reshape economic activity and organizational decision-making. My research asks how platform design, market mechanisms, and emerging intelligent technologies influence the creation, distribution and evaluation of value. I examine how these changes shape individual behavior and social interactions, organizational strategies and practices, and the distribution of market opportunities, with broader implications for societal welfare. I also study how AI transforms professional expertise, judgment, and influence, and how market and human outcomes inform platform design.

My empirical work draws on large-scale field data, econometrics and causal inference, field experiments, machine learning, and LLM-based methods. I am also interested in using game-theoretic analytics and structural modeling.

My work is centered on digital platforms across the gig and sharing economy, on-demand delivery, digital health, e-commerce, etc. I have established research collaborations with leading digital platform enterprises, and continue to seek new partnerships with platform enterprises and AI firms to help address pressing challenges in frontline management practice and develop novel theoretical insights from these practical contexts. I am also beginning to explore related settings beyond platforms, including how AI and other emerging technologies may reshape clinical and operational practices in traditional healthcare organizations.

01

Platform governance
& design

I study how platform governance and algorithmic design shape the strategies of complementors within platform ecosystems. My work examines how platforms orchestrate value creation, influence complementors’ incentives to innovate and adapt, and shape value capture and competitive dynamics across the ecosystem.

PLATFORM STRATEGY · ECOSYSTEM GOVERNANCE · VALUE CAPTURE
02

Digital market
dynamics

What brings people into digital markets, how do they interact and respond to one another, and what sustains their participation? I study entrepreneurial motivation, innovation, peer interactions, and the ways consumers adapt their behavior and assess the value that providers create.

PARTICIPATION · INNOVATION · INTERACTION
03

AI & human
agency

How does AI change expertise, judgment, and influence? I study how people decide alongside AI, how conversational systems reshape professional expertise and the division of work, and how AI influences collective interaction. Across healthcare and organizations, I examine the implications for human judgment, decision authority, and agency.
